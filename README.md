## What it does

Pick one of three fictional Google reviews for the fictional business "Willow Creek Dental Studio" (5-star praise, 4-star wait-time complaint, 2-star billing confusion), watch the AI draft a reply, switch between three tones (Warm & grateful, Professional, Apologetic & solution-focused), edit the reply, and post it (simulated) to see the outcome in seconds — followed by a booking CTA.

## Honest framing

This is a **scripted simulation**. No real reviews, replies, or bookings. All business and reviewer names are fictional; no real data is sent anywhere.
